MedicPreneur is a pan-African healthcare entrepreneurship movement and incubation platform active across 13 countries. We exist to close the gap between clinical training and business skills: teaching medical, pharmacy, optometry, and nursing students how to build sustainable enterprises, master financial literacy, and launch health startups without compromising their grades.

Our community is open to all healthcare students (undergraduate and postgraduate) and practicing clinicians across Africa. This includes medicine & surgery, pharmacy, optometry, nursing, medical laboratory science, dentistry, physiotherapy, and public health.

We currently have active chapters and student leadership teams in Nigeria, Ghana, Zambia, Zimbabwe, Kenya, Ethiopia, South Africa, Cameroon, Tanzania, Uganda, Mozambique, Egypt, and Rwanda, with expansion underway into additional nations.

Foundational masterclasses and community workshops are 100% free through sponsor partnerships. Advanced 10-session incubator cohorts (such as the Build Your Business Bootcamp) offer heavily subsidized tuition and merit-based scholarships through our partner organizations.

All sessions are delivered virtually on weekends (primarily Saturdays in the West Africa Time / WAT zone). Complete HD recordings, slide decks, and worksheets are uploaded to the learning portal within 4 hours of session completion so you can study around your call duties.

The Fellowship strictly admits only 15 to 20 exceptional African healthcare professionals per 6-month cycle. We look for validated health ideas, clear personal commitment, and strong problem-solving capabilities within the African healthcare ecosystem.

Ambassadors dedicate approximately 2 to 3 hours per week. Activities include sharing announcements with fellow student leaders, organizing webinar watch sessions, and liaising with their national chapter president.

Per our PRD technical specification, all event times are stated in West Africa Time (WAT / UTC+1) by default. When you register, the calendar invite automatically adjusts to your local device timezone (CAT, EAT, or SAST).
