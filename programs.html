Intensive bootcamps, skill accelerators, and enterprise development tracks structured specifically for African healthcare professionals and students.

FLAGSHIP INCUBATION PROGRAM

Build Your Business Bootcamp

A rigorous, hands-on 10-session venture acceleration program taking healthcare innovators from ideation and clinical validation to customer acquisition, compliance, and venture funding.

10 Live Intensive Sessions Mentorship with Doctors & Founders Investor Demo Day African Healthcare Case Studies
Apply for Next Cohort

Complete 10-Session Curriculum

Session 01

Ideation & Healthcare Needs

Identifying high-leverage pain points across African clinical systems, public health bottlenecks, and community wellness gaps.

Session 02

Customer & Clinical Validation

Testing hypotheses with patients, doctors, hospital administrators, and pharmacies before writing a line of code or leasing space.

Session 03

Sustainable Business Models

B2B vs B2C health models, recurring subscription care, tiered clinical pricing, and micro-insurance partnerships in Africa.

Session 04

Ethical Branding & Positioning

Navigating advertising restrictions in medical practice while building a compelling, trusted, patient-centric brand.

Session 05

Product Development & MVP

Building minimal viable healthcare products, pilot clinic services, or digital prototypes with minimal capital.

Session 06

Legal, Compliance & Regulations

Medical councils (MDCN, MDCG, HPCZ), corporate registration, clinical liability, patient data privacy, and IP protection.

Session 07

Ethical Sales & Client Acquisition

Closing high-ticket clinical retainers, corporate wellness accounts, and hospital supply agreements with professionalism.

Session 08

Financial Literacy & Unit Economics

Bookkeeping, cash flow projections, gross margins on health commodities, clinic overheads, and personal doctor wealth strategies.

Session 09

Digital Marketing & Distribution

Omnichannel patient education, search discoverability for local clinics, WhatsApp marketing funnels, and referral loops.

Session 10 • Finale

Demo Day & Investor Pitching

Presenting validated ventures to a panel of healthcare investors, senior clinicians, and seed stage venture partners.
