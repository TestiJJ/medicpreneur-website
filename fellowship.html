The MedicPreneur Fellowship

A flagship cohort program for 15–20 selected African healthcare professionals per cycle, combining high-growth venture acceleration with executive career pivot coaching.

COHORT PROGRAM • 6 MONTHS

Africa's Premier Health Founder Incubator

Co-founded in collaboration with Life Beyond Clinical Practice (LBCP), this fellowship is a first-of-its-kind continental initiative: no one else has integrated the full clinician-to-entrepreneur-to-career-pivot journey as a single program.

Selected fellows receive dedicated 1-on-1 mentorship, seed grant qualification, regulatory support across African health ministries, and direct introductions to global healthcare impact funds.

15–20 Fellows Strictly capped cohort for deep personal attention.
$10,000+ Pool Demo Day seed grants and non-dilutive pilot funding.
Advisory

Executive Venture Coaching

Bi-weekly strategic advisory with senior doctors who have built hospital chains, health-tech startups, and clinical laboratories across Africa.

13 Countries

Pan-African Distribution

Direct access to launch pilot projects across our 13+ country chapter network reaching over 1,000 clinicians and student leaders immediately.

Legal & IP

Regulatory & IP Fast-Track

Access to health law experts assisting with medical council approvals, patent protection, clinical trial ethics, and cross-border expansion.

Summit Stage

Global Demo Day

Pitch to international healthcare venture capital funds, angel syndicates, and philanthropic donors at the annual November Benin City Summit.

Eligibility Criteria

  • ✓ Background: Healthcare student (final/penultimate year) or graduated clinician (doctor, pharmacist, optometrist, nurse, lab scientist).
  • ✓ Geography: Citizen or resident of any African nation.
  • ✓ Venture Focus: Clear idea, prototype, or early revenue in health-tech, clinical services, diagnostics, or pharmaceuticals.
  • ✓ Commitment: Ability to commit 5–7 hours weekly for 6 months (compatible with clinical shift schedules).

Selection Timeline & Stages

Stage 1
Online Application & Deck Submission: Review of problem statement, team background, and African health market fit.
Stage 2
Shortlist & Video Interview: 25-minute interview with Founder Moses Adetoro and the Advisory Board.
Stage 3
Final Selection & Induction: Offer letters sent to the top 15–20 fellows with onboarding masterclass.
