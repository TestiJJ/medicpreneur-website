STUDENT LEADERSHIP CORPS

Lead the Healthcare Business Shift on Your Campus

MedicPreneur Ambassadors are student changemakers who understand that clinical training alone isn't enough. They mobilize peers, organize local watch parties for our webinars, represent our programs, and build the foundational chapters of tomorrow.

In return, ambassadors receive executive mentorship directly from Founder Moses Adetoro, full scholarship access to Academy courses, priority consideration for the Fellowship, and official leadership credentialing recognized across Africa.

Campus Leadership

What Ambassadors Do

  • • Host on-campus watch parties and workshops for MedicPreneur webinars.
  • • Share cohort announcements and masterclasses in medical student WhatsApp groups.
  • • Connect university leadership and medical associations to our national chapter leads.
  • • Pioneer local student healthcare venture discussions.
Perks & Growth

Exclusive Benefits

  • • Full scholarship access to MedicPreneur Academy cohort courses.
  • • Direct monthly mentorship calls with Founder Moses Adetoro.
  • • Fast-tracked consideration for the prestigious MedicPreneur Fellowship.
  • • Official certificate of leadership & recommendation letter for residencies/scholarships.
Requirements

Expectations & Requirements

  • • Currently enrolled healthcare student in any accredited African university.
  • • Active, credible presence within campus student bodies (FAMSA, OSAG, NOSA, etc.).
  • • Ability to commit 2–3 hours per week to campus engagement.
  • • Strong communication skills and enthusiasm for healthcare entrepreneurship.
